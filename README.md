## Hello there!

I'm a Multiplatform Software Development graduate from [Fatec Itaquera](https://fatecitaquera.cps.sp.gov.br/), currently working as a Technology Consultant at [Konia Tecnologia](https://konia.com.br/) and pursuing a Systems Development Technician course at [Etec](https://www.cps.sp.gov.br/etec/).

I got into programming because it's fundamentally a logic-based field. I enjoy solving puzzles, tackling problems, and constantly challenging my mind to find creative solutions.
